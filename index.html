09 · Privacy & Your Data

No account needed. VEX.FOCUS does not ask you to sign in, and it does not ask for your name, email or password.

Your study data stays on your device. Notes, flashcards, planner tasks, review topics and quiz scores are saved in your own browser on this device. They are not uploaded to a VEX.FOCUS server, and the creator cannot see them. Clearing your browser’s site data can delete them, so use “Export local backup” to keep a copy.

Local AI. Llama runs in your browser on your device, so your notes are processed there and are not sent to an AI company. The first time you use it, the model files are downloaded from the internet.

Photos and PDFs. Reading text from photos and PDFs also happens in your browser. The tools that do this are loaded from public online services, which can see your connection like any website you visit.

Visit statistics. This site uses Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights to count visits and measure page speed. They collect anonymous usage information, such as pages viewed, device type and country. VEX.FOCUS does not send your notes, flashcards or scores to these tools.
